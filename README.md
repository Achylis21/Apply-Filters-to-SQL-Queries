# Apply-Filters-to-SQL-Queries

## Project Summary
This project demonstrates the practical application of SQL to navigate, filter, and extract specific datasets from a relational database. It highlights how to manage and investigate organizational data by querying security logs and employee records. By utilizing a variety of SQL clauses and operators—including `WHERE`, `AND`, `OR`, `NOT`, `LIKE`, and `BETWEEN`—this project illustrates how to establish precise conditions to isolate data based on timeframes, geographical locations, and departmental affiliations.

## Key Scenarios Investigated
*   How can we retrieve after-hours failed login attempts?
*   How do we retrieve login attempts that occurred within a specific date range?
*   How can we retrieve all login attempts that originated outside of a specific country (e.g., Mexico)?
*   How do we retrieve a list of employees who work in the Marketing department at the East Office Building?
*   How can we retrieve employees who belong to either the Marketing or Sales departments?
*   How do we retrieve all employees who are not part of the Information Technology department?
